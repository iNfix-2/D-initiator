My role

Web developer and UI designer

Tools & approach

The brief

Move course discovery, application intake and admissions documents into one coherent experience for prospective aviation students.

The build

I designed the course catalogue and built a responsive admissions flow. Structured forms guide applicants through their details and document submissions.

The portal connects course information to the application process so a prospective student can move directly from reading a syllabus to applying.
