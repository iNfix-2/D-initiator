Project
DigiNorth
Type
Platform engineering
Role
Lead full-stack developer
Stack
Next.js, PostgreSQL, role-based access control

The brief

Connect hackathons, talent academies and startup programmes across the 19 states of Northern Nigeria.

The build

I structured the platform as 13 modules backed by PostgreSQL. Role-based access gives programme teams the tools they need to manage their own cohorts and submissions.

Organisers can admit a cohort, review a project and manage a state delegation from the same system.
