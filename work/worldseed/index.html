WorldSeed 3D.

A planet you can sculpt in the browser.

My role

Creative frontend architect

Tools & approach

Three.js, React Three Fiber, GLSL

The brief

Give people a way to shape a planet and explore its atmosphere directly in a web browser.

The build

I built the terrain with procedural noise in a vertex shader. A separate shader handles light scattering through the atmosphere, so the landscape and the light can change independently as you orbit the planet.

Touch controls let visitors orbit the same scene on a phone. Moving the terrain calculations onto the GPU keeps the interface free to respond to input.
